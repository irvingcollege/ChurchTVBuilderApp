## Dependency audit

Direct dependencies were upgraded to Astro 7.4.1, Netlify adapter 8.2.10 and Sharp 0.35.5, with the Sharp version consistently overridden through the dependency tree. Vitest was upgraded to 5.0.5 to remove test-worker advisories. `npm audit --omit=dev` still reports 12 high entries rooted in `braces` and `node-forge` through Netlify’s development emulation packages (`@netlify/vite-plugin`, images/ipx/listhen and functions-dev/zip-it-and-ship-it/fast-glob). The current registry provides no patched versions in those dependency lines. `npm audit fix` did not resolve them; its force suggestion downgrades the adapter to an older vulnerable release and was not used. Image/edge-function emulation is disabled, and none of this app’s request handlers invoke the affected tools. This reduces the relevant surface but does not remove the dependency advisories. Track upstream fixes and rerun the checks before public launch. A clean audit is **not** claimed.
